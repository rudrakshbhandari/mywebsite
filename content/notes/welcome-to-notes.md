I wanted a place on this site that felt more like a working notebook than a polished publication.

Some thoughts will stay short. Some will become essays. Some will start in tech and end up somewhere closer to life, philosophy, or politics.

The point is not to force every idea into a clean thesis. The point is to have a place where I can think in public without flattening everything into "content."
