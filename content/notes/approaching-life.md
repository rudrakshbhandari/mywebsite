Does everyone else also treat their life like an optimization problem? Or are some of us just wired like this (is it a STEM thing?).

If the only constant is the passage of time, shouldn't everything we do be centered around it? Should we be very conscious of how time is slipping away on a daily basis? I think I am, and it results in a great sense of urgency, but sometimes a sense of overwhelming restlessness.
