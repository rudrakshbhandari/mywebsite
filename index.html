About Me

I'm Rudraksh Bhandari, a second-year Computer Science major at University of California San Diego and former Software Development Engineer Intern at Amazon Web Services (Hyperplane team). I like building full-stack applications to solve real-world problems.

At Triton Software Engineering, I develop full‑stack features for the David Brower Center platform—a database for nonprofit organizations to visualize relationships between NPOs—using TypeScript, React, Node.js, Prisma, and Postgres.

I also serve as Sophomore Representative at Triton Quantitative Trading, leading outreach and seminars on risk management, game theory, and options strategies.
