Why notes, not a blog

This page is for ideas that are still alive. Some will be short. Some will turn into essays. Some will stay as fragments because that is the honest shape of the thought.

Working rules

  • Short by default.
  • Personal, not polished into LinkedIn voice.
  • Mixed topics, because real interests bleed into each other.
